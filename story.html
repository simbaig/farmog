Why We Started

We Couldn't Find Paneer We Could Trust. So We Made It.

Farm OG began with a simple frustration — the market was full of paneer, but none of it was pure. No clear sourcing, no testing, no accountability. We decided to fix that ourselves.

The Problem We Set Out to Solve

The market had gone silent on where its dairy came from

Walk into any market and you will find paneer everywhere. But ask where the milk came from, whether it was tested, or how long ago it was made — and you will get silence.

We started Farm OG because we were tired of that silence. As people who care deeply about what goes into food, we found it unacceptable that something as fundamental as dairy had become opaque, industrialised, and untrustworthy.

The answer was not to find a better supplier. The answer was to build the supply chain ourselves — starting at the farm.

Our Farmer Network

Built on Real Relationships

We work with a dedicated group of local farmers who share our belief that good dairy starts with healthy animals and honest practices. These are not anonymous suppliers — they are partners we know by name, whose farms we visit, and whose methods we understand.

Direct Collection

Milk is collected directly from our partner farms every morning. No middlemen, no holding tanks, no uncertainty about origin.

Known Sources

Every batch of milk is traceable to the farm it came from. We know exactly whose cows produced the milk in every product we make.

Long-Term Partnerships

We do not switch suppliers for price. Our farmer relationships are built on consistency, fair payment, and mutual accountability.

How We Make It

Same Day. Every Day.

From the moment milk arrives at our facility to the moment a product leaves it, every step is deliberate. We do not cut corners because we cannot afford to — our name is on every batch.

01

Milk arrives, testing begins

Every intake of milk is tested with a GC report before it enters production. Milk that does not meet our standards does not become a Farm Og product. Full stop.

02

Small-batch production

We produce in small batches — not because it is easier, but because it is the only way to maintain the quality control that large-scale production cannot offer. Every batch gets individual attention.

03

Made the same day

Everything we make is produced the same day the milk arrives. There is no cold storage of half-finished product, no carry-over from yesterday. Fresh means fresh.

04

Batch-level lab testing

Before any product leaves our facility, it is lab tested at the batch level. Two tests — one on the milk intake, one on the finished product. No shortcuts, no exceptions.

Two tests. Every batch. No shortcuts.

What We Stand For

Three promises behind every batch

Transparency

We test every batch and we are happy to show you the results. Our GC reports and lab certificates are not marketing — they are our standard operating procedure.

No Preservatives

Nothing we make contains preservatives, stabilisers, or additives. If it cannot be made purely from milk, it does not go in.

Accountability

Every product is traceable back to the batch it came from, the test it passed, and the farm the milk came from. That is the level of accountability we hold ourselves to.
