Delivery Windows

Delivery Schedule

We run two delivery windows daily so your paneer arrives at the right time for your kitchen.

6–9 AM

Morning Delivery

Ideal for restaurants opening for breakfast and lunch service. Order must be placed by 9:00 PM the previous evening.

4–7 PM

Evening Delivery

Perfect for dinner service and home kitchens. Order must be placed by 12:00 PM on the same day.

Minimum Order

Simple minimums, priority for bulk

Bulk orders above 20 kg receive priority scheduling. Contact us to set up a standing order.

5 kgper delivery for restaurant accounts
500 gper delivery for home orders
